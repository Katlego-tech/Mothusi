# Teacher questionnaire (Google Form), for sending 2026-11-05

Purpose: get the numbers and the stories Mothusi's pitch still lacks (high-value-targets.md
§5; the mentor's "watch, measure" and "demo is not proof"). Every question below maps to something we will use.
Nothing asks for learner names, marks or school names.

**Design rules**

- **10 minutes.** Teachers are the most time-poor people we will ask (TALIS: 7.1 h/week marking). Mostly tick-box.
  Questions marked ★ are the core 14; cut the rest first if the form must be shorter.
- **Mirror TALIS wording** where a question has a TALIS twin, so a respondent's answer can be set beside the
  national figure ([talis-2024-findings.md](talis-2024-findings.md)).
- **No personal data about learners, and nothing that identifies the school** unless the teacher chooses to
  offer it in the last section. Google Forms: turn **off** "collect email addresses"; ask for contact only in §9.
- **Don't mention AI until §7.** Earlier sections should measure the job, not the reaction to a technology.
- Settings: one response per device off (shared staffroom phones), progress bar on, shuffle off.

---

## Form title

**Marking and assessment: 10 minutes of your experience**

## Intro text (paste as the form description)

> I'm a former curriculum assistant. I worked as a curriculum assistant in a public high school, and I'm now building a tool
> to take marking and assessment paperwork off teachers' desks. Before building anything I want to understand
> how the work actually happens for you. This takes about 10 minutes. It is anonymous: it does not ask for your
> name, your school's name or anything about individual learners. The last section asks, optionally, whether
> you'd be willing to talk for 15 minutes or share a blank test paper. Thank you.
