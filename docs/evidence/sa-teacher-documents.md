# Documents South African public-school teachers work with

Research note for Mothusi (Colleague Zero, 13–14 Nov 2026). Written 2026-11-04 by the project researcher (via Claude Code). Scope: CAPS schools, Grades R–12, Department of Basic Education (DBE) and provincial education departments (PEDs). Private/IEB schools differ in detail but use the same families of documents.

**Mandated** = required by national policy (CAPS, the National Protocol for Assessment, SIAS, the QMS collective agreement, SASA) or by a standing provincial circular. **Practice** = universal in schools but shaped by the school or district. Items marked ⚠ need checking against the current year's circulars before Mothusi relies on their exact format.

> **Update 2026-11-04:** the ⚠ items are settled for 2026 in dbe-teachers-site.md §1.
> The 2023/24 ATPs are retained (Circular S19 of 2025) except for CAT and IT, which have an updated CAPS from
> Jan 2026. FET PoA weightings come from Circular S8 of 2023. The 2021 exam guidelines remain valid until 2027.

## 1. Curriculum and planning (what to teach, when)

| Document | What it is | Who produces it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CAPS** (Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statement) | The national curriculum per subject and phase: content, pacing, assessment requirements. One PDF per subject per phase. | DBE | Mandated |
| **ATP** (Annual Teaching Plan) | Week-by-week sequencing of CAPS content and the formal assessment tasks for the year, per subject and grade. Revised/"recovery" ATPs issued since 2020; DBE says they apply "until repealed". ⚠ Check the 2026 set. | DBE, sometimes adapted by PEDs | Mandated |
| **Work schedule / pacesetter** | Older term for the same thing; some provinces still issue pacesetters per term. | PED / district | Practice |
| **Lesson plans** | Daily or weekly plans derived from the ATP: objectives, activities, resources, differentiation, assessment. Format set by the school or district; DBE issues exemplars (e.g. Foundation Phase). | Teacher | Mandated in principle (kept in the teacher file); format is practice |
| **Teacher's / subject file** (also "educator portfolio") | The ring binder (or folder) that holds everything below: CAPS extract, ATP, lesson plans, assessment programme, tasks and memos, mark sheets, moderation reports, intervention records, circulars. Inspected by HODs, subject advisers and moderators. | Teacher | Mandated (National Protocol for Assessment: "Teacher Portfolio … all planning for assessment … tasks and tools … Record Sheet(s)") |
| **Timetable** (class, teacher, invigilation) | Period allocation per class and teacher. | School | Practice |
| **LTSM lists and requisitions** | Textbooks, DBE workbooks, stationery ordered per grade; textbook retrieval registers at year end. | Teacher → HOD → school | Practice (provincial retrieval policies) |
| **DBE workbooks** (Rainbow workbooks) | Learner workbooks for Languages and Maths, Grades R–9, two volumes a year. | DBE | Mandated supply |

## 2. Assessment (how learning is measured)

| Document | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **POA / PoA** (Programme of Assessment) | The year's formal assessment tasks per subject and grade: task, form (test, project, oral, practical…), marks, weighting, date. Derived from CAPS §4 and the ATP; the school consolidates one PoA per grade. | Mandated (National Protocol for Assessment) |
| **School Assessment Policy** | How the school implements the protocol: recording, reporting, moderation, missed tasks, re-tests. | Mandated (school must have one) |
| **SBA** (School-Based Assessment) tasks, with **marking guidelines/memoranda** and **rubrics** | Each formal task: question paper or brief, memo or rubric, cognitive-level grid (Bloom's/CAPS levels), analysis grid. Grade 12 SBA is externally moderated and counts 25% of the NSC. | Mandated |
| **Assessment task cover sheet / task grid** | Task name, date, total, weighting, cognitive levels, moderation sign-off. | Practice (provincial templates) |
| **Mark sheets / record sheets** | Per-task marks for every learner, converted and weighted; signed by teacher, HOD and (FET) subject adviser; often also captured in SA-SAMS. Codes for absent/incomplete work. | Mandated |
| **Mark schedules / promotion schedules** | End-of-year consolidated marks per learner per subject and the promotion/progression decision. Generated from SA-SAMS. | Mandated |
| **Learner report cards** (quarterly) | Per-term report per learner: marks, levels (1–7), comments, attendance. Generated from SA-SAMS or a school template. | Mandated (four reports a year) |
| **Item analysis / error analysis / diagnostic analysis** | After a test: per-question performance, common errors, and what to re-teach. Required after Grade 12 trials and often after common tests. | Practice (required in some provinces) |
| **Moderation tools and reports** | Pre-moderation (task checked before learners write) and post-moderation (sample of scripts re-marked) by HOD, then district/province; moderation report forms with findings and signatures. | Mandated (SBA moderation framework; Umalusi for Grade 12) |
| **Common / provincial / national question papers and memos** | Provincially set tests (e.g. Grade 9 common tests, Grade 12 June/September trials), past NSC papers and memos used for revision and exemplar tasks. | Supplied by PED/DBE |
| **Exam timetables, invigilation registers, irregularity reports** | For internal and NSC exams. | Mandated (NSC) |
| **Learner evidence / learner portfolio / learner file** | Each learner's completed formal tasks, kept for moderation. | Mandated |
| **Umalusi and DBE exam circulars ("Assessment Instructions", AssIn)** | Provincial numbered instructions each year that change weightings, dates, codes. ⚠ The current year's set is the authority on format. | Mandated |

## 3. Learner support and inclusion

| Document | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **SIAS forms** (Policy on Screening, Identification, Assessment and Support, 2014) | **SNA 1** Teacher assessment and intervention (teacher with parent). **SNA 2** School-based support team (SBST) review and support plan. **SNA 3** District-based support team (DBST) decision. **DBE 120/121/126** forms for placement and concessions. | Mandated |
| **ISP** (Individual Support Plan) | The learner's support plan that comes out of SNA 1/2: barriers, goals, strategies, accommodations, review date. | Mandated under SIAS |
| **Learner profile** | A record that follows the learner through schooling: personal details, health, attendance history, support needs, achievement per year. DBE template; kept by the school. | Mandated |
| **Intervention / remedial / enrichment plans and records** | Lists of at-risk learners per subject, planned interventions, extra classes, evidence of outcome. HODs and districts ask for these each term. | Practice (strongly expected) |
| **Progression and retention records** | Learners progressed (not promoted) under the progression policy, with the support offered. | Mandated |
| **Concession applications** (exam accommodations) | Extra time, scribe, reader, spelling concession etc., with assessments attached. | Mandated (DBE concessions policy) |
| **Referral letters** (psychologist, social worker, clinic, SBST) | Referral and feedback forms. | Practice |

## 4. Classroom administration and compliance

| Document | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Class register and period register** | Daily attendance (class register) and per-period attendance (secondary). Captured in SA-SAMS; attendance drives funding and NSC eligibility. | Mandated (Policy on Learner Attendance) |
| **SA-SAMS** (South African School Administration and Management System) | The DBE's school database: learners, staff, timetable, marks, reports, attendance, LURITS submission. Teachers capture marks and attendance into it or hand them to the admin clerk. | Mandated in public schools |
| **Learner admission forms and enrolment documents** | Admission form, birth certificate/ID copies, proof of address, immunisation, previous report, transfer card. | Mandated (admission policy) |
| **Code of conduct, discipline records, incident reports, detention/merit registers** | Per the school's code of conduct (SASA). | Mandated (code) / practice (registers) |
| **Parent communication** | Letters/notices, consent forms (excursions, photos, data), parent-meeting registers, communication logs, WhatsApp/SMS notices. | Practice |
| **Homework diaries / homework policy** | School-set. | Practice |
| **Excursion and sport documents** | Approval forms, indemnity, transport, risk assessment. | Mandated by PED for excursions |
| **Safety and health** | Fire drill and evacuation records, first-aid incident register, OHS checks, nutrition (NSNP) registers where applicable. | Mandated |
| **Circulars and minutes** | DBE and PED circulars, district memos, staff and departmental meeting minutes, SMT instructions. | Practice (file them) |
| **Stock / inventory** | Classroom asset register, textbook stock. | Practice |

## 5. Professional and performance documents

| Document | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **QMS** (Quality Management System for school-based educators, ELRC Collective Agreement 2 of 2020; replaced IQMS) | Annual performance cycle: Personal Growth Plan (PGP), lesson observations, appraisal instrument, scores, development plan. ⚠ Check whether PEDs have moved fully from IQMS to QMS this year. | Mandated |
| **PGP** (Personal Growth Plan) | Teacher's own development targets for the year. | Mandated (QMS) |
| **SACE registration and CPTD** (Continuing Professional Teacher Development) | SACE certificate; CPTD points log (Teacher's Professional Development Portfolio) over three-year cycles. | Mandated (SACE Act) |
| **Job description, PAM** (Personnel Administrative Measures), **leave forms, PERSAL documents** | Employment admin. | Mandated |
| **School Improvement Plan (SIP), School Self-Evaluation (SSE), Academic Performance Improvement Plan (APIP)** | School-level plans teachers contribute to and are measured against. | Mandated (Whole School Evaluation) |
| **Subject improvement plan / subject policy** | HOD- or teacher-written plan for the subject's results. | Practice |

## What this means for Mothusi

The documents that eat the most teacher time, recur every term, and have fixed formats a digital colleague can read and produce are: **lesson plans from the ATP** (1), **PoA, SBA tasks with memos/rubrics, mark sheets and item analysis** (2), **report comments** (2), **intervention plans and SIAS/ISP paperwork** (3), and **the teacher file as a whole** (1). Every one of them is anchored to a public DBE document (CAPS, ATP, National Protocol for Assessment, SIAS), which gives an agent a ground truth to check its own work against.
